# Book Identity & Style Guide

## Core Purpose
To compile our studies on the intersection of software engineering and the restored gospel of Jesus Christ, demonstrating that the laws of creation, covenants, and refinement apply identically to both human-AI collaboration and personal discipleship.

## Formatting & Design Constraints (KDP Specifications)
*   **Trim Size:** 6.0" x 9.0"
*   **Mirrored Margins:** Inside gutter is 0.75" (or 0.8" in CSS), outside margin is 0.5", top/bottom margin is 0.75". This ensures a clean binding for 100–150 page paperbacks.
*   **Running Headers:** Chapter title on right pages, book title (*Beyond the Prompt*) on left pages, page numbers centered at the bottom, headers/footers suppressed on the first page of each chapter.
*   **Body Typography:** Garamond or Georgia (11.5pt, 1.5 line height, justified, paragraphs indented 1.5em, first paragraph after a header unindented).

## Writing Principles (Strict Constraints)
1.  **Modular Study Format:** Every chapter must contain:
    *   **Binding Question:** A single, sharp question the chapter answers.
    *   **Anchor Passage:** A blockquoted scripture or prophetic citation.
    *   **The Core Reframe:** The scripture/doctrinal analysis.
    *   **The Engineering Parallel:** The concrete software/AI workflow analogy.
    *   **Becoming Commitment:** Practical personal action items.
2.  **Voice & Tone:** Deep, direct, unadorned, personal, warm. Avoid generic summaries, presenter tics, or meta-narration of the document's structure (e.g. "In this chapter, we will look at...").
3.  **Transitions:** Sections and paragraphs connect by causation (*therefore* or *but*) rather than sequence (*and then* or *first/secondly*).
4.  **Verification:** Every scripture quote and general conference reference must be linked to its online/local gospel library path.
