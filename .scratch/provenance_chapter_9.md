That phrase appears in neither verse. The 2026-05-28 Claude Code audit caught it; Michael ratified rewriting the anchor to cite Ether 12:20 alone (the verse that directly names the brother of Jared and carries the chapter's binding question). v.19 is now referenced in the chapter prose but no longer presented as part of a continuous quotation.
