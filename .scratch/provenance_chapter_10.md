1. **Alma 12:10** (anchor; also quoted in the Core Reframe) — "And therefore, he that will harden his heart, the same receiveth the lesser portion of the word; and he that will not harden his heart, to him is given the greater portion of the word, until it is given unto him to know the mysteries of God until he know them in full." — 🟢 exact (full verse as anchor; the fragment "he that will not harden his heart, to him is given the greater portion of the word" reused in the Core Reframe is exact).
2. **1 Nephi 2:16** — "And it came to pass that I, Nephi, being exceedingly young... he did visit me, and did soften my heart that I did believe all the words which had been spoken by my father; wherefore, I did not rebel against him like unto my brothers." — 🟢 exact.
3. **Ezekiel 36:25–26** — "Then will I sprinkle clean water upon you, and ye shall be clean: from all your filthiness, and from all your idols, will I cleanse you. 4. **Alma 22:18** — Core Reframe: "I will give away all my sins to know thee"; Becoming #1: "wilt thou make thyself known unto me, and I will give away all my sins to know thee" — 🟢 both exact fragments of "...wilt thou make thyself known unto me, and I will give away all my sins to know thee, and that I may be raised from the dead, and be saved at the last day."
5. **Helaman 3:35** — "Nevertheless they did fast and pray oft, and did wax stronger and stronger in their humility, and firmer and firmer in the faith of Christ... which sanctification cometh because of their yielding their hearts unto God." — 🟢 exact.
6. **Job 23:16** — "For God maketh my heart soft, and the Almighty troubleth me" — 🟢 exact (chapter drops the trailing colon).
