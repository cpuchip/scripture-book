## Direct Quotes & Scripture Citations

### 1. Abraham 4:18 (Anchor Passage)
*   **Verified canonical text:** "And the Gods watched those things which they had ordered until they obeyed."
*   **Quoted in manuscript with an inserted comma:** "And the Gods watched those things which they had ordered, until they obeyed."
*   **Source:** [Abraham 4:18](../../gospel-library/eng/scriptures/pgp/abr/4.md)
*   **Manuscript status:** ✅ substantively exact; comma insertion is typographical.

### 2. Abraham 4:10, 4:21, 4:31 (trust gradient quotes)
*   **Verified canonical text v.10 (full clause):** "and the Gods saw that they were obeyed."
*   **Verified canonical text v.21 (full clause):** "And the Gods saw that they would be obeyed, and that their plan was good."
*   **Verified canonical text v.31 (full clause):** "We will do everything that we have said, and organize them; and behold, they shall be very obedient."
*   **Quoted portions in manuscript:** "the Gods saw that they were obeyed" (v.10); "saw that they would be obeyed" (v.21); "behold, they shall be very obedient" (v.31).
*   **Source:** [Abraham 4](../../gospel-library/eng/scriptures/pgp/abr/4.md)
*   **Manuscript status:** ✅ exact partials.

### 3. Abraham 5:2 — 🔴 **MANUSCRIPT DISCREPANCY**
*   **What the manuscript says:** *"In Abraham 5:2, we read that on the seventh day the Gods rested and 'counseled among themselves.'"* (attributing the phrase "counseled among themselves" to v.2)
*   **Verified canonical Abraham 5:2 in full:** "And the Gods said among themselves: On the seventh time we will end our work, which we have counseled; and we will rest on the seventh time from all our work which we have counseled."
*   The phrase "counseled among themselves" does NOT appear in Abraham 5:2. It appears in [Abraham 4:26](../../gospel-library/eng/scriptures/pgp/abr/4.md) ("And the Gods took counsel among themselves") and [Abraham 5:3](../../gospel-library/eng/scriptures/pgp/abr/5.md) ("their decisions at the time that they counseled among themselves to form the heavens and the earth").
*   What v.2 actually describes: rest *from* work they had counseled (past tense). The seventh-day act is rest, not council.
*   **Source:** [Abraham 5:2](../../gospel-library/eng/scriptures/pgp/abr/5.md)
*   **Manuscript status:** 🔴 needs revision. Either (a) reframe the paragraph to say "the seventh day is named as rest *from* the work they had counseled" or (b) move the "session of reflection" framing to a different scriptural anchor. The underlying theological point (sabbath as evaluation moment) is sound; it just isn't *in* Abr 5:2.
