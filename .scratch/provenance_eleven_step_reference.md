# Provenance: Eleven-Step Creation Cycle Reference Page

*New artifact 2026-05-27 per council ratification (T2). Authored by Claude Opus 4.7 against canonical sources.*

## Source Materials & Studies
*   **The eleven-step creation cycle** is documented across the workspace in [docs/work-with-ai/guide/05_complete-cycle.md](../../../docs/work-with-ai/guide/05_complete-cycle.md), [.spec/covenant.yaml](../../../.spec/covenant.yaml), and the teaching workstream's Episode 2 script ([teaching/episodes/02-the-four-disciplines.md](../../../teaching/episodes/02-the-four-disciplines.md), 2026-05-23). The cycle maps the Moses/Abraham creation accounts to a complete creative process — from intent to Zion.
*   **The seven verbs for the framework-untouched steps** (binding, entrusting, observing, recovering→re-aiming, resting, giving, harmonizing) come from the 2026-05-23 Episode 2 script Section 4, which itself derived them from Abraham 4–5.
*   **Step 8 "Atonement — re-aiming"** verb sharpened from "recovering" per Michael's 2026-05-27 council pointer to [study/hope-and-the-grammar-of-pairs.md](../../../study/hope-and-the-grammar-of-pairs.md) §III: *"Repentance, in this frame, is not first a re-doing of behavior but a re-aiming of the eye"* (line 129) and *"The discipline is repentance — turning the eye back, again and again, to the prototype"* (line 175). The verb shift from passive (recovering) to active vision-discipline (re-aiming) is doctrinally sharper.
*   **The four-vs-seven mapping** (engineering disciplines = steps 1, 4, 5, 6; framework-untouched = 2, 3, 7–11) is established in the Episode 2 script Section 4 and in the book's own Chapter 2.

## Scriptural Anchors
*   The cycle as a whole derives from Abraham 4–5. The seventh-step "watching" specifically anchors to [Abraham 4:18](../../gospel-library/eng/scriptures/pgp/abr/4.md): "And the Gods watched those things which they had ordered until they obeyed."
*   The cycle's structural pattern (spiritual creation before physical) is also explicitly given in [D&C 29:32](../../gospel-library/eng/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29.md) and [Moses 3:5](../../gospel-library/eng/scriptures/pgp/moses/3.md).
