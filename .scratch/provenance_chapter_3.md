## Direct Quotes & Scripture Citations

### 1. Moses 3:5 (Anchor Passage)
*   **Verified canonical text (v.5 in full):** "And every plant of the field before it was in the earth, and every herb of the field before it grew. For I, the Lord God, created all things, of which I have spoken, spiritually, before they were naturally upon the face of the earth. For I, the Lord God, had not caused it to rain upon the face of the earth. And I, the Lord God, had created all the children of men; and not yet a man to till the ground; for in heaven created I them; and there was not yet flesh upon the earth, neither in the water, neither in the air;"
*   **Quoted portion (book uses `...` to skip the rain-clause):** "For I, the Lord God, created all things, of which I have spoken, spiritually, before they were naturally upon the face of the earth. ... And I, the Lord God, had created all the children of men; and not yet a man to till the ground; for in heaven created I them; and there was not yet flesh upon the earth..."
*   **Source:** [Moses 3:5](../../gospel-library/eng/scriptures/pgp/moses/3.md)
*   **Manuscript status:** ✅ exact partial; ellipsis correctly omits the "had not caused it to rain" middle clause.

### 2. Doctrine and Covenants 29:32 *(not 29:31 as the prior provenance had it)*
*   **Verified canonical text (v.32 in full):** "First spiritual, secondly temporal, which is the beginning of my work; and again, first temporal, and secondly spiritual, which is the last of my work—"
*   **Quoted portion:** "First spiritual, secondly temporal, which is the beginning of my work"
*   **Source:** [D&C 29:32](../../gospel-library/eng/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/29.md)
*   **Manuscript status:** ✅ quote exact; citation in manuscript is correct (29:32). Prior provenance file had the verse number wrong (29:31).
*   **Enhancement note:** the second half of v.32 ("and again, first temporal, and secondly spiritual, which is the last of my work") names a *bidirectional* pattern — spec-first on the way in, lived-experience-spiritual on the way out — that could enrich Ch 3 if surfaced. Council item, not a fix.
