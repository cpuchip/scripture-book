## Webster 1828 Definitions

### *spirit* (n.) — quoted from senses 3 and 4
*   **Verified canonical text (sense 3):** "Life, or living substance, considered independently of corporeal existence; an intelligence conceived of apart from any physical organization or embodiment; vital essence, force, or energy, as distinct from matter."
*   **Verified canonical text (sense 4):** "The intelligent, immaterial and immortal part of man; the soul, in distinction from the body in which it resides…"
*   **Verified against:** `mcp__webster__webster_define` workspace MCP tool, 2026-05-26.
*   **Source for reader:** [1828.ibeco.me/word/spirit](https://1828.ibeco.me/word/spirit).
*   **Manuscript status:** ✅ all three phrases the book attributes to Webster appear exactly as quoted.
