## Direct Quotes & Scripture Citations (re-verified via gospel_get 2026-06-02)

### 1. D&C 131:7–8 — anchor passage
*   *Quote:* "There is no such thing as immaterial matter. All spirit is matter, but it is more fine or pure, and can only be discerned by purer eyes; We cannot see it; but when our bodies are purified we shall see that it is all matter."

*   *Verdict:* 🟢 exact.

### 2. D&C 88:22
*   *Quote:* "For he who is not able to abide the law of a celestial kingdom cannot abide a celestial glory."
*   *Canonical:* "For he who is not able to abide the law of a celestial kingdom cannot abide a celestial glory." — 🟢 exact.

### 3. D&C 88:34
*   *Quote:* "that which is governed by law is also preserved by law and perfected and sanctified by the same"
*   *Canonical:* "And again, verily I say unto you, that which is governed by law is also preserved by law and perfected and sanctified by the same." — 🟢 exact fragment (drops the leading "And again, verily I say unto you,").

### 4. D&C 88:35
*   *Quote:* "cannot be sanctified by law, neither by mercy, justice, nor judgment"
*   *Canonical:* "That which breaketh a law... cannot be sanctified by law, neither by mercy, justice, nor judgment. Therefore, they must remain filthy still." — 🟢 exact fragment.

### 5. D&C 88:6
*   *Quote:* "comprehended all things, that he might be in all and through all things, the light of truth"
*   *Canonical:* "He that ascended up on high, as also he descended below all things, in that he comprehended all things, that he might be in all and through all things, the light of truth;" — 🟢 exact fragment.

### 6. D&C 88:67  *(rebuild addition)*
*   *Quote:* "If your eye be single to my glory, your whole bodies shall be filled with light... and that body which is filled with light comprehendeth all things"
*   *Canonical:* "And if your eye be single to my glory, your whole bodies shall be filled with light, and there shall be no darkness in you; and that body which is filled with light comprehendeth all things." — 🟢 exact (drops leading "And"; ellipsis honestly elides "and there shall be no darkness in you;").

### 7. Alma 7:11–12
*   *Quote (direct):* "that his bowels may be filled with mercy, according to the flesh, that he may know according to the flesh how to succor his people according to their infirmities" (the surrounding "suffering pains, afflictions, and temptations of every kind" is paraphrased, not quoted).
*   *Canonical v12:* "...that his bowels may be filled with mercy, according to the flesh, that he may know according to the flesh how to succor his people according to their infirmities." — 🟢 exact fragment.

### 8. 3 Nephi 24:3
*   *Quote:* "shall sit as a refiner and purifier of silver; and he shall purify the sons of Levi, and purge them as gold and silver, that they may offer unto the Lord an offering in righteousness"
*   *Canonical:* "And he shall sit as a refiner and purifier of silver; and he shall purify the sons of Levi, and purge them as gold and silver, that they may offer unto the Lord an offering in righteousness." — 🟢 exact fragment (drops leading "And he").

### 9. Matthew 6:22  *(rebuild addition)*
*   *Quote:* "The light of the body is the eye: if therefore thine eye be single, thy whole body shall be full of light."
*   *Canonical:* identical. — 🟢 exact.
