## TL;DR

The book is in good shape and the rebuilds were clean — **the fact-check found zero quote drift across 28+ verified citations.** The single biggest move is the one nobody could have seen before the rebuilds: **the glossary now contradicts the chapters it's supposed to summarize** (it endorses microservices for Zion, which Ch 12 refutes; it maps heart-softening to cache/memory-locks, the exact parallel Ch 10's rebuild threw out; and it contains a flatly false technical claim — "overfitting → compilation errors"). Four passes flagged it independently. The most credible page is the **fabricated-quote production note in Ch 6** — both human-persona readers and the brainstorm named the book's vulnerability as its credibility engine. The most strained parallel is **Chapter 9's engineering half** — three passes called it the longest and least load-bearing.

The deepest convergence isn't a single fix; it's a posture: **every method independently said the book is strongest where it admits the limit of its own analogy, and weakest where it presses hardest.** Protect the limits, vary the wording, and the book gets truer without losing an ounce of sincerity.

## Tier 1 — Convergence items (multi-pass; recommend ratifying)

### T1.1 — Reconcile the glossary with the rebuilt chapters (+ fix a false claim)  ⟵ 4 passes
**Finding.** `15_glossary_of_fused_terms.md` predates the chapter rebuilds and now fights them: **Zion** = "synchronized microservices architecture" (Ch 12 calls microservices the *wrong* model); **Heart-Softening** = "invalidating stale cache connections… releasing memory locks" (Ch 10's rebuild *removed* that parallel for gardening + the mirror); **Veil** = "API gateway." Plus the AI-engineer reader caught a hard error: **Dross** = "overfitted parameters that cause *compilation errors*" — overfitting does not cause compilation errors; this is false and a technical reader will not forgive it.
**Move.** Rewrite the engineering side of each glossary entry to match the current chapters (Zion → shared-intent ward-council multi-agent; heart-softening → yielding assumed-clarity / the mirror; fix or cut the overfitting line; reconsider the "fused-terms" entries the engineer reader said "pun instead of map"). Verify every glossed term is actually used in the body.
**Cost.** Small–medium. **Source.** Editor 1.3 · Gospel-reader · AI-reader · Editing-research.

### T1.2 — Tighten Chapter 9 (especially its Engineering Parallel)  ⟵ 3 passes
**Finding.** Ch 9 is ~2,950 words, nearly double the next-longest. It walks the three postures AND the four groups in doctrine, then re-walks both in engineering register. The gospel-reader lost the thread; the AI-reader called the engineering half the weakest in the book ("the Group-2-engineer-drops-the-discipline story has zero dependency on how LLMs actually work — decorative").
**Move.** Tighten the Engineering Parallel's re-statement of the three postures and four groups (the reader just read them in the Core Reframe); cut the decorative restatement, keep the one load-bearing engineering instance. Consider whether the doctrine half wants a trim too.
**Cost.** Medium. **Source.** Editor 2.4 · Gospel-reader · AI-reader.

### T1.3 — Keep the limit-naming, vary the wording ("the analogy must break to be trusted")  ⟵ 4 passes
**Finding.** The imago-Dei close ("The shape rhymes. The substances do not… The same Author wrote both… only one of them is a soul") is the book's **genre antibody** — editing-research named it as exactly the move *The Tao of Physics* was faulted for lacking; the AI-reader said his leaning-in happened *only* where the author admitted the limit; the brainstorm's "microtonal detuning" says the same. BUT the editor pass found the close is now near-**verbatim** across Ch 5/8/10/11 — a repeated stamp, which the voice rules ban.
**Move.** Keep the move in every chapter that has it (do not soften for flow — this is the antibody), but vary the phrasing so it lands as a returning theme, not a copy-paste. Same for the templated Engineering-Parallel *openings* ("Scripture has carried X for millennia…" in Ch 8/10/11) and the "older than the tool" tic.
**Cost.** Small. **Source.** Brainstorm · AI-reader · Editing-research (keep) + Editor 2.1/2.2/2.3 (vary).

### T1.4 — Fix the "walk each step in turn" overpromise  ⟵ 1 pass (mine), but structural & concrete
**Finding.** The reference page (`00_eleven_step_reference.md:19`) promises the chapters "walk each one in turn," but they're ordered thematically: Watching (step 7) comes before Covenant (step 2); two chapters carry *Atonement* (8 and 10) with *Context* (Ch 9, step 5) wedged between; and Ch 9's *Context* tag doesn't match its actual content (hope/posture/pride).
**Move.** Reword the reference page to say the chapters explore the steps thematically (not sequentially); add the step *number* to each italic chapter tag so the non-linear mapping is visible; differentiate or re-label the two *Atonement* chapters and reconsider Ch 9's tag.
**Cost.** Small. **Source.** Editor 1.1/1.2/2.5.

## Tier 1+ — Brainstorm-led (worth a council decision on scope)

### T1+.A — The dual-audience problem may have a *structural* answer  ⟵ 3 passes
**Finding.** TRIZ, brainwriting, and mind-mapping converged: don't write every chapter for both audiences equally — assign each a primary audience and let the other receive it as translation (marginal tracks / two reading protocols). The gospel-reader's version: in the heaviest doctrinal chapters (0, 8, 12) *box off* the engineering half the way Ch 2's sidebar already does, and surface the glossary at the front. The editing-research version: the preface is doing four jobs in 30 lines.
**Move (two scopes).** *Small:* box the engineering halves in Ch 0/8/12, add a front-of-book glossary pointer, trim the preface's four jobs. *Large:* a true two-track layout across the book — the brainstorm's disney lens gave the vivid exemplar, a **Talmud-style page** (Abraham 4–5 in the outer margin, the engineering narrative in the center column) so "the layout becomes the fusion it argues for." The small version is cheap and convergent; the large version is a real restructure (and a hard typesetting problem for KDP).
**Cost.** Small / Large. **Source.** Brainstorm · Gospel-reader · Editing-research. **→ RQ-3.**

### T1+.B — Vulnerability is the credibility engine; elevate it  ⟵ 4 passes
**Finding.** The brainstorm's dominant theme ("stop defending, start exposing"), the AI-reader ("the confession is the strongest thing in the book"), the gospel-reader ("the production note earned my trust on the whole book"), and editing-research all point one way. The existing Tier 1.2 (Ch 6 production-note visual prominence) is the concrete lever.
**Move.** Elevate the Ch 6 and Ch 11 production-notes visually (boxed/page-break — the typography item), and consider a forward-reference earlier. Do NOT "open the book with the failure" — that re-opens the just-ratified front-loading fix (see Rejects).
**Cost.** Small (mostly typography). **Source.** Brainstorm · AI-reader · Gospel-reader · Editing-research.
