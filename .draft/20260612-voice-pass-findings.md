### THE SYSTEMIC ONE (emerging by p1_05, confirm across walk)

**V-01 ★ Inversion density (the "not X; Y" spine).** Each practice legitimately
coins ONE inversion as its principle ("a plan is a map, not a debt"; "the bound
is not a cage"). The drift: *incidental* sentences also take the inversion shape,
so practices stack 4–7 negation-contrast structures in ~20 lines. Individually
earned; collectively the Opus drumbeat Michael named. Discipline to propose:
keep each practice's coin (body + Remember box), convert incidental inversions
to affirmative/colon shapes. Per-file instances listed below.
