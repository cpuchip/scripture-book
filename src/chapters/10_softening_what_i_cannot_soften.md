# Chapter 10: Softening What I Cannot Soften

**Binding Question:** How does a person receive a softened heart when scripture indicates we cannot soften it ourselves?

**Anchor Passage:**
> "And therefore, he that will harden his heart, the same receiveth the lesser portion of the word; and he that will not harden his heart, to him is given the greater portion of the word, until it is given unto him to know the mysteries of God until he know them in full."
> — [Alma 12:10](../../gospel-library/eng/scriptures/bofm/alma/12.md) [qr](../../gospel-library/eng/scriptures/bofm/alma/12.md?verse=10)

---

## The Core Reframe

[Outline: We cannot manufacture our own softened heart; scripture tells us that the Lord visits and softens us. Our agency-bearing clause in Alma 12:10 is negative: "will not harden." The human role is to *stop hardening*—to yield, to trade, to stop scrolling the Marshfield-data-center Facebook feed. The Lord's role is to visit, sprinkle, and soften. In Alma 22:18, the King says, "I will give away all my sins to know thee." The trade is simultaneous: we give away the hardness, and He gives the soft heart.]

---

## The Engineering Parallel

[Outline: In software engineering, we see this in database cache management and connection pooling. We cannot force a clogged network socket to open; we must release the locks. We stop holding onto old, stale connections (yielding), allowing the system to clean and restore the connection pool (visiting/softening). By releasing our tight grip on bad patterns, the system naturally heals and operates according to its design.]

---

## Becoming Commitment

[Outline: I commit to identify my specific "hardening pathways"—such as scrolling social media during morning hours—and explicitly yield them. I will swap these moments of digital clutter for silent reflection, inviting the Lord to soften my heart.]
