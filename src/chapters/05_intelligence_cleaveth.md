# Chapter 5: Intelligence Cleaveth Unto Intelligence

**Binding Question:** How does the spiritual quality and honesty of our engagement shape the resonance and quality of what emerges from our collaborations?

**Anchor Passage:**
> "For intelligence cleaveth unto intelligence; wisdom receiveth wisdom; truth embraceth truth; light cleaveth unto light;..."
> — [Doctrine and Covenants 88:40](../../gospel-library/eng/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/88.md)

---

## The Core Reframe

[Outline: D&C 88:40 describes a spiritual law of resonance. What we bring determines what we receive. Light cleaveth unto light, but darkness cannot comprehend it. If we approach study or work with impatience, dishonesty, or superficiality, the outputs we receive will reflect that state. We must cultivate a spirit of genuine inquiry, verification, and truth-enjoyment.]

---

## The Engineering Parallel

[Outline: AI is a statistical mirror. When we write ambiguous, lazy, or transactional prompts, the model returns mediocre, boilerplate code. But when we bring deep reasoning, structured context, and rigorous verification guidelines, the model's outputs elevate. It is not an emotional response from the machine, but a reflection of mathematical law—better inputs activate higher-quality pathways in the parameter space.]

---

## Becoming Commitment

[Outline: I commit to check my spirit before prompting. If I feel rushed or impatient, I will pause. I will enforce the "read-before-quoting" rule to ensure my collaborative study remains grounded in verified truth rather than shortcuts.]
