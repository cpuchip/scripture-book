# Appendix: Point Your AI Here

The practices in this book are not meant to stay on the page. Your AI assistant can learn them in its first session.

We built a free companion kit for exactly that: a small folder of instructions any capable model can follow — Claude, GPT, Gemini, or whatever comes next — plus templates for the working files this book keeps returning to: the intent, the covenant, the journal, the living state.

**[github.com/cpuchip/ai-jumpstart](https://github.com/cpuchip/ai-jumpstart)** [qr](https://github.com/cpuchip/ai-jumpstart)

To use it, put the kit next to your project and tell your assistant: *Read AGENTS.md in the ai-jumpstart folder and follow it. This is our first session.* The assistant will ask what you are building, sharpen the vision with you before anything is built, ask for its bounds, and set up the memory files so that the next session, or a different AI entirely, arrives already knowing what the two of you have made. The cycle this book describes, installed where the work happens.

The kit is shared the way this book is shared: freely. And it improves the way everything else in this book improved — by being tried on real models and corrected where they drift. If yours drifts, tell us.
