# Recommended Study

For readers who desire to deepen their study of these patterns, the following curated scriptures, prophetic messages, and study notes provide the primary theological and practical foundations for the principles in this book.

---

## Scriptural Anchors

*   **The Metaphysics of Truth:** [Doctrine and Covenants 93](../../gospel-library/eng/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/93.md) — Establishes the physical continuity of light, truth, and intelligence, outlining how disobedience subtracts light.
*   **The Law of Glory:** [Doctrine and Covenants 88](../../gospel-library/eng/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/88.md) — Outlines the absolute necessity of law in preserving, perfecting, and quickening spirit-matter.
*   **The Enoch Cycle:** [Moses 7](../../gospel-library/eng/scriptures/pgp/moses/7.md) — Details the 365-year process of Zion's construction, the grief of a weeping God, and the ultimate consolidation of unity.
*   **The Seer's Geometry:** [Ether 2–3](../../gospel-library/eng/scriptures/bofm/ether/2.md) — Models the three stewardship problems (Prescription, Rest, and Proposal) and the Brother of Jared's face-to-face seership.
*   **The Civilization Manual:** [3 Nephi 11–28](../../gospel-library/eng/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/11.md) — Records Jesus' personal blueprint for building the happiest society on earth, starting with the death of contention.
*   **The Realized Zion:** [4 Nephi 1](../../gospel-library/eng/scriptures/bofm/4-ne/1.md) — Documents the daily practices (fasting, prayer, and gathering) that sustained the Nephite Zion and the pride-driven sequence of its fall.

---

## Prophetic Messages

*   **The Challenge to Become (October 2000):** Elder Dallin H. Oaks — Explains that the Final Judgment is not an audit of deeds but a physical recognition of what we have become.
*   **Clean Hands and a Pure Heart (October 2007):** Elder David A. Bednar — Distinguishes between the redeeming power of the Atonement (cleansing hands) and its enabling power (purifying the heart).
*   **Come to Zion (October 2008):** Elder D. Todd Christofferson — Outlines the three requirements for building Zion today: unity, holiness, and caring for the poor.
*   **Keep Walking, and Give Time a Chance (April 1997):** Sister Virginia H. Pearce — Details the patient, iterative nature of personal and community sanctification.
