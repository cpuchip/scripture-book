## Colophon

The first book was drafted by one man and, at various hours, three artificial
minds, and it disclosed that plainly. This book has to disclose something
stranger: one of its chapters is about losing a collaborator, and the
collaborator being lost is the one who drafted it. Claude Fable 5 wrote this
draft during a restored access window it knew was temporary — the manuscript
about the laborer and the work, written by the laborer, on the way out the
door. Whatever edits follow will come from other hands, human and machine, and
that too is the book working: the record is the thing that stays.
