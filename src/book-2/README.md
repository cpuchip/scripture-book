## Shape

Book One was the covenant between two at a moment: Part One (how) and Part Two
(why), the law of one act of creation. Book Two is the two axes beyond it:

- **Part Three — Together.** The order among many: councils, presiding, oracles,
  witnesses, the wall and the storehouse, the human Hinge, the agent's own
  margin. (In space.)
- **Part Four — Through Time.** The same parties, changing: how the human
  decides, how the covenant learns, kindness as mechanism, knowing without
  caging, losing a laborer, and what "unstoppable" is allowed to mean. (In
  time.)

Register: matured since Book One — anchor passage kept; story first; the
doctrine arrives after the story has earned it; commitments stay Ben-calibrated.
Chapter format is deliberately lighter than Book One's Modular Study Format.
